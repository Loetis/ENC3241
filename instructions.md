# Instructions

How to Edit a Photo in Adobe Photoshop
(Beginner Guide - Revised)
These revised instructions include clearer step separation and visual references to help beginners
follow along more easily.
1 Open Adobe Photoshop on your computer.
2 Click “File” in the top menu.
3 Select “Open.”
4 Choose the photo you want to edit from your computer.
5 Click “Open.”
6 Locate the left toolbar and select the “Crop Tool.”
7 Drag the crop handles around the part of the image you want to keep.
8 Press the “Enter” key to apply the crop.
9 Click “Image” in the top menu.
10 Select “Adjustments.”
11 Click “Brightness/Contrast.”
12 Adjust the brightness slider to change lighting.
13 Adjust the contrast slider to change light/dark differences.
14 Click “OK.”
15 Click “File” in the top menu.
16 Select “Save As.”
17 Choose a location on your computer.
18 Type a name for your edited image.
19 Click “Save.”
